## Research basis and limits

The [W3C Flexbox specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-flexbox/) distinguishes
intrinsic item sizing, line collection and cross-axis alignment. This supports
measuring complete peers before packing, not independently stretching leaves.
Our row-break DP optimizes bounded contiguous rows; it is not a full
Knuth–Plass paragraph algorithm. [CSS Text Level 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-text-4/)
describes balanced wrapping separately from ordinary wrapping; it does not prove
that changing a box width in a different renderer improves our test designs.
Research analogies inform candidate design; frozen output comparisons and actual
glyph measurements decide acceptance.

## Visual regression correction

An initial broad text-frame tightening experiment changed menu selection and
separated prices in a skyscraper output despite zero automated flags. It was
removed before publication. Frame trimming is now limited to a blocked,
source-evidenced primary-axis translation of a single text atom and requires
identical rendered ink bounds/wraps. A regression test reproduces the original
snapping drift; another exercises this narrowly gated translation. Interrupted
trial snapshots are not presented as current review results.

The fallback raw-frame correction has a direct conference-fixture regression:
every atomic group is fitted uniformly into its grid slot, all IDs remain, and
exported raw/rotated frames stay inside 320×50. It does not assert that long
speaker names can be made legible at that size.

### Remaining limitations

The conference 320×50 still selects a fallback and retains six flag types:
text-on-text, control collision, text/art occlusion, text/control clearance,
glyph overflow and structural error. Raw-frame fitting fixes `_safe_grid` in
isolation, but subsequent realization still expands the title off-canvas in
this final output. The exact structural error is retained in the audit; this
release does **not** claim a fully bounded final conference design.

The unflagged festival strip still has a fragmented day sequence and very small
secondary type. The improved festival skyscraper has large headline/control
contrast in size, a 2/1/2/2 control wrap and an uneven final performer row.
These are aesthetically imperfect, although the previously flagged collisions
and clearance failures are removed. No safety gate was disabled to get zero
flags. Some measured macro alternatives lose the full raster contrast test even
when their geometry-only precheck passes; they are not silently promoted.
